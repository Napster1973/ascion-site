Who we are

Unlimited Imagination.

A friendly, small and passionate team making comics that kids will love.

Paris, France

Stories made with care, from first sketch to final page.

Ascion is a newly founded media agency based in Paris, dedicated to producing high-quality 2D comic books for children.

We work alongside independent authors, each with their own stories and worlds, and bring their projects to life with the craft of a studio and the heart of a small team.

  • 2D comic books
  • Independent authors
  • For children

Our mission: to change the way children discover stories, by giving them worlds they won't find anywhere else.
